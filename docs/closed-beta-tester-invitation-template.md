# Recovery Companion Closed Beta ? Tester Invitation

Use this as the basis for an email or message to an approved tester.

---

Thank you for helping test Recovery Companion.

This is a closed Android beta, so you may encounter bugs or unfinished
areas. Your feedback will help identify problems before broader release.

## How to join

1. Use the Google account that has been approved for the beta.
2. Join the Recovery Companion beta Google Group when invited.
3. Open the Google Play beta opt-in link provided separately.
4. Choose to become a tester.
5. Install Recovery Companion from Google Play.

If Google Play says you are not eligible, first confirm that:

- you are signed into the approved Google account
- you have joined the beta Google Group
- you are opening the correct closed-test link

## After installing

Open:

**More > Closed Beta Tester Guide**

That page explains what to test and how to protect sensitive recovery
information when reporting a problem.

For feedback, open:

**More > Beta Feedback & Support**

Useful feedback tells us:

- what happened
- what you expected
- how to reproduce it
- whether the problem happened again

Please avoid including private journal entries, Step Work, recovery
contact names, chat text, or other sensitive recovery information unless
it is essential to understanding the problem.

Recovery Companion is recovery-support software. It is not a medical
device, professional treatment, or an emergency/crisis-response service.

Thank you for helping improve the beta.
