# Closed Beta Tester Roster Policy

## Repository rule

Do not commit tester names, email addresses, Google account addresses, or
other personally identifying tester roster information to this
repository.

Tester membership is administered through the approved Google Play /
Google Group access mechanism.

## Allowed repository information

Repository documentation may contain:

- generic tester roles
- beta process instructions
- track names
- tester-count goals
- non-identifying test scenarios
- release/version identifiers

## Not allowed

Do not commit:

- tester email addresses
- tester phone numbers
- tester recovery history
- tester diagnoses or treatment information
- tester Google account identifiers
- screenshots containing private tester information
- exported Play Console tester lists

## Removal

A tester leaves the beta by being removed from the external beta-access
group/list.

Source code changes are not required merely because tester membership
changes.
