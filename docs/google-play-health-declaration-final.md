# Google Play Health Apps Declaration

## Declaration

Recovery Companion provides health-related functionality.

Select:

**Mental and Behavioral Health**

Recovery Companion provides addiction-recovery support, including:

- recovery check-ins
- journaling
- recovery goals and routines
- fellowship/contact support
- Step Work
- recovery reviews and insights
- reminders
- optional AI-supported reflection and recovery conversation

## Medical-device status

Recovery Companion is **not a medical device**.

The app:

- does not diagnose disease or a medical condition
- does not provide medical diagnosis
- does not prescribe treatment
- does not claim to cure or prevent disease
- does not replace licensed clinical care
- is not an emergency or crisis-response service

Do not select Medical Device Apps unless Recovery Companion later
develops functionality that actually qualifies as a regulated medical
device.

## User-facing safeguards

The application contains Privacy & Health Information explaining that:

- Recovery Companion is recovery-support software
- AI responses can be inaccurate
- the application does not replace professionals or trusted human
  recovery relationships
- the application is not an emergency service

## Privacy

Recovery Companion handles potentially sensitive addiction/recovery
information.

The public privacy policy is:

https://afyoung3083.github.io/recovery-companion/privacy/

Before Play submission, verify the URL is publicly accessible without
authentication.
