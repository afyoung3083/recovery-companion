# Recovery Companion Closed Beta Tester Onboarding

## Purpose

The closed beta is intended to validate Recovery Companion with a
small group of real users before broader distribution.

The beta should produce evidence about:

- installation and update reliability
- first-run onboarding clarity
- core recovery workflow usability
- local-data preservation
- reminders
- optional AI reflection and chat
- privacy expectations
- confusing or misleading behavior
- crashes and functional defects

## Tester prerequisites

A tester should:

1. Use an Android device compatible with the beta.
2. Use the Google account invited to the testing group.
3. Accept the Google Play closed-testing invitation.
4. Install Recovery Companion through the Play testing link.
5. Keep the app updated during the beta.
6. Understand that beta software may contain defects.

## Before testing

Testers should review:

- Closed Beta Tester Guide
- Privacy & Health Information
- Beta Feedback & Support

Recovery Companion is recovery-support software, not a medical device
or emergency/crisis-response service.

## Privacy expectations

Do not ask testers to provide sensitive recovery information merely to
report a bug.

Feedback should normally contain:

- what happened
- what was expected
- reproduction steps
- safe beta diagnostics

Feedback should normally exclude:

- journal text
- Step Work
- chat content
- names of recovery contacts
- API tokens
- unnecessary personal or recovery information

## Recommended beta scenarios

Each tester should attempt:

1. Install the beta.
2. Complete first-run onboarding.
3. Configure at least one recovery goal or routine.
4. Complete a Daily Recovery check-in.
5. Create and retrieve a Journal entry.
6. Add or review a Fellowship contact.
7. Configure a reminder if useful.
8. Review Recovery Insights.
9. Try an optional AI reflection if comfortable doing so.
10. Submit at least one usability observation through Beta Feedback &
    Support.
11. Install at least one beta update and confirm local records survive.

## Feedback triage

Classify incoming reports as:

- crash
- data loss
- security/privacy
- functional bug
- AI behavior
- usability/confusion
- feature suggestion
- documentation/onboarding

Data-loss and security/privacy reports receive highest priority.

## Distribution rule

The signed Android App Bundle is uploaded to Google Play.

Testers install the app through the Google Play closed-testing flow.

Do not distribute the upload keystore or `key.properties` to testers.
