# Closed Beta Release Notes

Welcome to the Recovery Companion Android closed beta.

This beta includes:

- local-first encrypted recovery records
- Daily Recovery check-ins
- Journal
- goals and routines
- Fellowship tools
- Step Work
- Weekly and Monthly Recovery Reviews
- Recovery Insights
- optional AI reflection and companion chat
- private device reminders
- first-run onboarding
- privacy and data-ownership controls
- beta tester guidance and feedback tools

Please report bugs, confusing experiences, and suggestions through:

**More > Beta Feedback & Support**

Avoid including sensitive recovery details in feedback unless they are
necessary to reproduce the problem.
