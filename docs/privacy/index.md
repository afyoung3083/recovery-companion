# Recovery Companion Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 28, 2026**

Recovery Companion is an addiction-recovery support application designed
around a local-first privacy model.

This policy explains what information Recovery Companion handles, when
information may leave your device, and the choices available to you.

## Recovery information stored on your device

Core Recovery Companion records are designed to be authoritative on your
device.

These records may include information you choose to enter into features
such as:

- Daily Recovery check-ins
- Journal
- goals and routines
- Fellowship contacts
- Step Work
- Weekly Recovery Reviews
- Monthly Recovery Reviews
- Recovery Insights
- profile and sobriety information
- reminder preferences

Recovery Companion uses protected local application storage for
authoritative recovery records.

Android application backup is disabled for Recovery Companion.

## Information sent for optional online features

Recovery Companion includes optional features that require communication
with an online service.

Information may leave your device when you explicitly use features such
as:

- Recovery Companion chat
- AI Journal reflection
- AI Daily Recovery reflection
- AI Recovery Insights reflection
- AI Weekly Review reflection
- AI Monthly Review reflection

When you explicitly request one of these features, information needed to
provide that feature is transmitted to the Recovery Companion service.

For AI reflection features, Recovery Companion is designed to send the
specific entry, check-in, review, or summary associated with the
reflection you requested rather than automatically uploading your entire
local recovery history.

When you use companion chat, conversation information is transmitted so
the service can respond in context.

Saving or viewing a core recovery record locally does not by itself
constitute a request to send that record for AI analysis.

## Sensitive information

Addiction-recovery information can be highly sensitive.

You control what information you enter into Recovery Companion and
whether you invoke optional online AI features.

Avoid entering information that you do not want processed as part of the
feature you are using.

## Beta feedback

Recovery Companion may provide beta feedback tools that generate
technical diagnostic information.

The diagnostic report is designed not to automatically include
authoritative recovery records, journal entries, chat text, or API
tokens.

You should review feedback before sending it and remove any information
you do not want to share.

## Data export and deletion

Recovery Companion provides controls for managing application-owned
recovery information.

Depending on the available application version, these controls may
include the ability to:

- prepare an export of Recovery Companion recovery records
- permanently delete Recovery Companion-owned recovery data from the
  device

Deleting the application may also remove application-local data according
to Android platform behavior.

## Security

Recovery Companion is designed to protect authoritative recovery records
using encrypted local storage.

Information transmitted to online Recovery Companion services is intended
to use encrypted network transport.

No software system can guarantee absolute security.

## AI limitations

AI-generated responses may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate
for your circumstances.

Recovery Companion's AI features are support tools. They should not
replace your own judgment, trusted human relationships, recovery
fellowship, professional care, or other appropriate support.

## Health and medical disclaimer

Recovery Companion provides addiction-recovery support tools.

Recovery Companion is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat,
cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

Recovery Companion does not replace a physician, licensed mental-health
professional, therapist, sponsor, clergy member, or other qualified
professional.

Recovery Companion is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact appropriate
local emergency or crisis services.

## Children

Recovery Companion is not intentionally designed to collect personal
information from children through a public child-directed service.

The intended audience and Google Play age-distribution settings must
remain consistent with the application's published store configuration.

## Third-party service processing

Optional online functionality may rely on infrastructure or service
providers acting on behalf of Recovery Companion.

The exact retention and service-provider treatment applicable to
production online processing is reviewed as part of the application's
Data Safety and provider configuration process.

This policy does not claim that optional online processing is ephemeral
unless that behavior has been verified for the production service.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may change as Recovery Companion develops.

Material changes should be reflected in the published policy and, when
appropriate, application or release information.

## Contact

Questions about Recovery Companion privacy can be directed through the
support or feedback contact published with the Recovery Companion Google
Play listing.

A dedicated public privacy contact may be added as beta distribution
infrastructure is finalized.
