# Recovery Companion ? Google Play Store Listing

## App name

Recovery Companion

## Short description

Private, local-first tools for daily addiction recovery and reflection.

## Full description

Recovery Companion helps you organize the daily practices, reflection,
and relationships that support addiction recovery.

The app is designed around a local-first model: your core recovery
records remain authoritative on your device.

### Daily recovery tools

Use Recovery Companion to:

- complete Daily Recovery check-ins
- keep a private recovery Journal
- track recovery goals and routines
- organize Fellowship contacts
- work through Step assignments
- set device-based reminders
- review Weekly and Monthly Recovery summaries
- explore Recovery Insights

### Optional AI support

When you choose to use an AI-powered feature, Recovery Companion can
help you reflect on a selected journal entry, check-in, recovery summary,
or conversation.

AI is optional. Saving or viewing your local recovery information does
not automatically send your entire recovery history for AI analysis.

AI responses can be inaccurate or incomplete and should support?not
replace?your judgment, sponsor, fellowship, therapist, clergy, medical
professionals, or other trusted people.

### Privacy-focused beta

Recovery Companion is designed to minimize unnecessary exposure of
sensitive recovery information.

Core recovery records use protected local storage, and Android
application backup is disabled.

The app also includes:

- recovery-data export and deletion controls
- Privacy & Health Information
- privacy-conscious beta diagnostics
- clear disclosure before optional AI use

### Important

Recovery Companion is an addiction-recovery support application.

It is not a medical device, does not provide diagnosis or medical treatment,
and is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact appropriate
local emergency or crisis services.

This release is part of a closed beta intended to identify bugs,
confusing workflows, and opportunities to improve Recovery Companion
before broader release.
