# Google Play Health Apps Declaration ? Draft

## Recommended health feature

Select:

**Mental and Behavioral Health**

Google describes this category as including tools for mental-health
support, counseling services, and addiction-recovery programs.

Recovery Companion's addiction-recovery functionality fits this
category.

## Product characterization

Recovery Companion:

- supports personal addiction recovery
- provides journaling, check-ins, goals, routines, reviews, fellowship,
  reminders, and optional AI-supported reflection
- is not a medical device
- does not diagnose disease
- does not provide medical diagnosis or medical treatment
- is not an emergency or crisis-response service
- is intended to support, not replace, qualified professionals and
  trusted human recovery relationships

## Before submitting

Verify that Play Console wording still matches Google's current Health
Apps declaration form.

Do not select Medical Device Apps unless Recovery Companion later
develops regulated medical-device functionality.

If app functionality materially changes, reevaluate the declaration.
